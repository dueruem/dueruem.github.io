Official Player Guidelines & Privacy Policy

Terms of Service Conduct Privacy Policy Legal & Liability
TL;DR: Matches can take 2 hours. We use automated scripts to delete outdated data. By joining, you consent to being streamed. You must be 16 years old or older or have parental consent.

1. Terms of Service

1.1 Obligation to Finish

By using the /join command, you acknowledge that a CS2 match can last up to two hours. You are obligated to play the match in its entirety. Abandoning results in a Strike.

1.2 Abandonment Criteria

  • No-Show: Failure to connect within 10 minutes.
  • Time-Out: Disconnected for a cumulative 2 minutes.
  • Withdrawal: Explicitly stating intent to leave.

1.3 Competitive Integrity

Cheating or "griefing" is strictly prohibited. We review GOTV replays to verify violations. Punishments include blacklisting.

1.4 Account Linking

To participate in custom matches, you must link the same Steam account you will be playing with to your Discord account using the /link command.

2. Communication & Conduct

2.1 Behavioral Standards

No hate speech or harassment. Toxicity that ruins the experience for the other 9 players or the stream audience will result in immediate removal.

2.2 Stream Consent & Safety

By participating, you grant Knice Kustoms permission to broadcast and archive your gameplay, in-game name, and voice comms on platforms like Twitch. No advertising or offensive avatars allowed.

3. Privacy Policy (GDPR)

We process data under Art. 6(1)(b) (Service Fulfillment) and Art. 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interest) of the GDPR.

3.1 Technical Processing

We collect your SteamID and DiscordID. Our custom bot processes this data to send RCON commands to our game server for team management and access control.

3.2 The 90-Day "Janitor" Rule

Per the principle of data minimization, our Janitor Scripts run every 15 minutes to purge data:

  • Strikes: Deleted after 30–90 days of clean play.
  • Replays: Permanently deleted after 90 days unless under active investigation.
  • Bans: Expired ban records are purged; only active bans are retained.

3.3 The /unlink Command

To exercise your "Right to be Forgotten," use /unlink. This immediately deletes your linked profile and associated stats (excluding active security bans).

4. Legal Notice & Liability

4.1 Disclaimer of Liability

Knice Kustoms is a community project provided "as-is." We are not liable for any technical malfunctions, data loss, or damages to hardware/software resulting from the use of our bot, plugins, or servers.

4.2 Age Requirement & Parental Consent

Users must be at least 16 years of age to participate. If you are between the ages of 13 and 15, you may only participate if your legal guardian has reviewed and agreed to these Terms and our Privacy Policy on your behalf. Participation by anyone under the age of 13 is strictly prohibited.
