Official Player Guidelines & Privacy Policy

Terms of Service Conduct Privacy Policy Legal & Liability
TL;DR: Matches can take 2 hours. We use automated scripts to delete outdated data. By joining, you consent to being streamed and recorded. You must be 16 years old or older or have parental consent.

1. Terms of Service

1.1 Obligation to Finish

By using the /join command, you acknowledge that a CS2 match can last up to two hours. You are obligated to play the match in its entirety. Abandoning results in a Strike.

1.2 Abandonment Criteria

  • No-Show: Failure to connect within 5 minutes.
  • Time-Out: Disconnected for a cumulative 2 minutes or one full round (counting from end of freezetime to start of next freezetime).
  • Withdrawal: Explicitly stating intent to leave.

If your internet may not be fast enough to download workshop maps in time to connect, consider downloading the Workshop Collection in advance. This collection contains all community maps the server is able to use.

1.3 Competitive Integrity

Cheating or "griefing" is strictly prohibited. We review GOTV replays (Demos) to verify violations. Punishments include blacklisting.

1.4 Account Linking

To participate in custom matches, you must link the same Steam account you will be playing with to your Discord account using the /link command.

1.5 Sharing Server Credentials

Sharing server connection details (password, connect command, connect URL, etc.) with non-participants is strictly prohibited, will result in those who join via shared details being banned, and is grounds for a permanent ban for the leaker.

1.6 The Strike System

Each strike is a formal record of a violation. We use a progressive system to encourage fair play:

Violation Count Consequence
1st Strike Warning Only
2nd Strike 1 Day Ban
3rd Strike 7 Day Ban
4th Strike 14 Day Ban
5th Strike 30 Day Ban
6th Strike 60 Day Ban
7th+ Strike 90 Day Ban

*Reminder: Clean play for 30-90 days removes strikes from your record (See Section 3.2).

2. Communication & Conduct

2.1 Behavioral Standards

No hate speech or harassment. Toxicity that ruins the experience for the other 9 players or the stream audience will result in immediate removal.

2.2 Stream & Demo Consent

By participating, you grant any players permission to broadcast and archive your gameplay, in-game name, and voice comms on platforms like Twitch. Furthermore, you acknowledge that all matches are recorded via GOTV and published as downloadable demos on our website for transparency and competitive review.

3. Privacy Policy (GDPR)

We process data under Art. 6(1)(b) (Service Fulfillment) and Art. 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interest) of the GDPR.

3.1 Technical Processing

We collect your SteamID and DiscordID. Our custom bot processes this data to send RCON commands to our game server for team management and access control.

3.2 The Janitor Rule (Data Retention)

Per the principle of data minimization, automated scripts run periodically to purge data:

  • Match Demos (GOTV): All match recordings, including player names, gameplay data, and chat logs, are made publicly available and are permanently deleted after 30 days.
  • Strikes: Deleted after 30–90 days. (Strike 1 after 30 days, Strike 2 after 60 days, Strikes 3+ after 90 days).
  • Bans: Expired ban records are purged; only active bans are retained for security purposes.

3.3 The /unlink Command

To exercise your "Right to be Forgotten," use /unlink. This immediately deletes your linked profile and associated stats (excluding active security bans).

4. Legal Notice & Liability

4.1 Disclaimer of Liability

Knerd Kollektiv is a community project provided "as-is." We are not liable for any technical malfunctions, data loss, or damages to hardware/software resulting from the use of our bot, plugins, or servers.

4.2 Age Requirement & Parental Consent

Users must be at least 16 years of age to participate. If you are between the ages of 13 and 15, you may only participate if your legal guardian has reviewed and agreed to these Terms and our Privacy Policy on your behalf. Participation by anyone under the age of 13 is strictly prohibited.
