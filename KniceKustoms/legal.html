Official Player Guidelines & Privacy Policy

Terms of Service Conduct Privacy Policy
TL;DR: Don't join if you have less than 2 hours of time. We use automated systems (Janitors) to delete your data every 90 days. Respect the other 9 players.

1. Terms of Service

1.1 Obligation to Finish

By using the /join command, you acknowledge that a CS2 match can last up to two hours (including overtimes). You are obligated to play the match in its entirety. If you abandon a match, you will be replaced and receive one Strike.

1.2 Abandonment Criteria

A match is officially considered abandoned if any of the following occur:

  • No-Show: Failure to connect to the server within 10 minutes of the match initialization.
  • Time-Out: Being disconnected for a total cumulative time of 5 minutes.
  • Withdrawal: Explicitly stating an intent to leave or "rage quitting."

1.3 Competitive Integrity

Unauthorized third-party software ("cheats") or intentional "griefing" (damaging teammates/throwing) is strictly prohibited. Staff review GOTV replays (also known as 'demos' or '.dem files') to verify violations. Punishments range from temporary strikes to permanent blacklisting.

2. Communication & Conduct

The rules of our Discord and Twitch communities apply to the game server at all times.

2.1 Behavioral Standards

No hate speech, discrimination, or harassment. We maintain a "chill" environment. Toxicity that ruins the experience for the other 9 players or the stream audience will result in removal.

2.2 Stream Safety

  • No Promotion: No advertising social media, gambling sites, or other servers.
  • Names/Avatars: Players must not have offensive names or avatars that violate Twitch/Discord ToS.
  • Doxing: Sharing personal information of others is a terminal violation.

3. Privacy Policy

We believe in "Privacy by Design." We only collect what is necessary to run the matches.

3.1 Data We Collect

We store your SteamID and DiscordID to link your profile. We also record GOTV Match Replays (.dem) to verify fair play and abandonment strikes.

3.2 The 90-Day "Janitor" Rule

We do not keep long-term records of your play history. Our automated Janitor Scripts run every 15 minutes to perform the following:

  • Strikes: Forgiven and deleted after up to 90 days of clean play (30 days for 1 Strike, 60 days for 2 strikes and 90 days for 3 or more).
  • Replays: All match recordings are permanently deleted from our archive after 90 days, unless they are currently subject to an active investigation by Staff.
  • Bans: Only active bans are retained to prevent evasion. Expired bans are purged.

3.3 Your Control (The /unlink Command)

You have the "Right to be Forgotten." At any time, you may use the /unlink command. This will immediately delete your linked profile and all associated stats from our database (excluding active bans and strikes).
